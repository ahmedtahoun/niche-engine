## The first ads

### Objective and format
- **Campaign:** Engagement → **Messaging apps → WhatsApp** ("click-to-WhatsApp"). In Egypt this usually beats sending people to a new website: people ask questions first, then order with cash on delivery.
- **Placements:** Advantage+ (Instagram + Facebook feeds, Stories, Reels).
- **One campaign, one ad set, 3 ads** (the 3 posts). After 3 days Meta shows which one wins, and you move the budget to it.

### Audience
- **Location:** start with **Cairo and Giza** only (fastest delivery, cheapest returns). Add Alexandria after the first 20 delivered orders.
- **Age:** 22–50, all genders.
- **Advantage+ audience on**, with a suggestion: coffee, Turkish coffee, espresso, coffee machines, cafés.
- **Exclude:** people who already messaged you (after week 1).

### Budget
- **Test:** EGP 300–500 a day for 3 days (about EGP 1,000–1,500). Don't change anything during these 3 days.
- **What to watch:**

  | Metric | Meaning | Rough target to check after 3 days |
  |---|---|---|
  | Cost per conversation | What you pay for each WhatsApp chat | EGP 10–25 is usually healthy for food |
  | Conversation → order | Share of chats that become orders | 20–30% if you answer within 5 minutes |
  | Cost per order | Ad spend ÷ orders | Must stay well under your profit per bag |

  These targets are rough. Your own first 3 days of results matter more than any benchmark.
- **Then:** double the budget on the winning image, stop the weakest, and keep a new image in testing every week.

**Speed matters most:** an ad chat answered in under 5 minutes converts several times better than one answered in an hour. Turn ads off when nobody can reply (late night).

## Before you spend: legal and practical checks
- **Food sales:** selling packaged coffee in Egypt normally needs a commercial register, a tax card and food-safety registration (NFSA). Small online sellers often start before having all of it, but **Bosta and Meta can ask for a commercial register later**, and so can a payment provider. Start the paperwork this week.
- **Bags:** use bags with a **one-way valve and a zip** if you can. Fresh coffee releases gas, and valve bags keep it fresh and look professional. Stick the label on, or use the round stamp sticker plus a handwritten date.
- **Ad review:** Meta reviews ads in a few minutes to a few hours. Health claims ("helps you lose weight") get ads rejected, and we use none.
- **Photos:** in week 2, shoot 10 phone photos in daylight: bag, beans in hand, kanaka pouring, espresso shot. Use them as new ads; real photos usually beat designs.
